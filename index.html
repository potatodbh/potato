Rulebook

EconNation is a 2–4 player classroom board-game style simulation. Players make economic decisions and answer questions to learn course concepts from CIE3M.

Goal

Finish with the best overall economic outcome (high GDP + high approval, while keeping unemployment and inflation under control).

GDP ↑ is good Approval ↑ is good Unemployment ↓ is good Inflation ↓ is good Treasury helps flexibility

What Happens Each Turn

  • An event automatically appears every few seconds.
  • Each player chooses an option by clicking their name under the option.
  • If all players choose early, the event resolves immediately.
  • If time runs out, missing players auto-pick so the game keeps moving.

Event Types

QUESTION (Quiz / Mixed)

  • One option is clearly correct.
  • Correct answers give a small bonus (classroom motivation).
  • Wrong answers give a small penalty.

DECISION (Policy / Trade-off)

  • No perfect choice.
  • Each option has pros and cons (economic consequences).

Winning & Ranking

  • Game ends when the timer hits 0.
  • Players are ranked by overall score (GDP + Approval − penalties).
  • Teachers can also assess learning by “Topics Covered” and player discussion.

Classroom Use Tip

After each event, pause briefly and ask: Which economic concept was involved and why?

Setup (Teacher-Friendly)

  • Pick a scenario mode (Full Curriculum or Unit Focus).
  • Choose 2–4 players and enter names.
  • Recommended: 20 minutes, events every ~9 seconds, decision timer 12 seconds.
  • Students should explain their reasoning out loud before choosing.
